# Falsification record — "a product's specification is five questions answered in three perspectives, every statement cited" (`../../rules/specs.md`, `../../rules/statements.md`; dyad-system #723)

**Claim (plan #723, revisions 1–3):** a specification craft is needed, and its form is one file per subject with five
questions — WHAT, WHY, HOW, WHEN, WHO — each answered in the three perspectives of one product, PRD, Architecture and
UX/UI, every statement cited or marked. Disposed by the done-`Y` of #723 (Rule-9 Form).

## The evaluation (#723's intent reading)
The craft play-book (`dyad/playbooks/craft-instantiation.md`) gave:
- **Trigger (`dmc:craft-trigger`): body of work.** No installed craft practised a product's specification.
- **Criteria (`dmc:craft-instantiation`): none fire.** D1: no sensitive class. D2: the same operator. D4: the first
  specifications refer to the ledger's rows. D5: no lifecycle of its own. D6: the `craft` zone fits.
- **Source (`dmc:craft-source`): authored.** No craft corpus of the practice existed to import.

What already held parts of the practice, and why none of it is the specification:

| # | part | class | ground |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | a specifications craft | new | no craft, play-book or record held a product specification's form |
| S2 | WHAT / WHY / HOW | overlap | the brief relay form, the board report (#298, #336) and the plan file present one d-work to the operator; a specification describes a product across rows |
| S3 | WHEN | new | release timing decides when a cut is made, `order: required` orders one plan's steps; neither says when a capability is due |
| S4 | WHO | overlap, partial | the board's WHO is a unit and holder; a product's users and segments are the chronicle craft's segmentation records, which a specification cites |
| S5 | PRD | overlap | the chronicle craft's lean canvas holds problem, segments, value and metrics, with no requirement or acceptance criterion |
| S6 | Architecture | overlap | sysarch holds this machine's engineering as Tended Rules, which a specification cites and never restates |
| S7 | UX/UI | new | nothing held an interface or a journey; #722's journey is the first |
| S8 | whose need | settled | for any operator's product (Q1), the first instance this system's own |

Recurrence (Rule-13 property 1): answering these questions by hand recurred four times as presentation (#298, #324,
#327, #335) and twice as product work (#721, #722).

## Attacks on the rules
| # | Attack | Result | Survivor |
|---|--------|--------|----------|
| F1 | **The board's WHAT/WHY/HOW/WHO already is this craft.** | **Refuted** | The board presents one row to the operator and ends with that row; a specification describes a product across rows and outlives any one of them. |
| F2 | **The lean canvas is the PRD.** | **Refuted** | It reads problem and segments for marketing and holds no requirement or acceptance criterion; a specification cites it for WHY and WHO (`../../docs/writing.md`). |
| F3 | **sysarch is the architecture specification.** | **Refuted** | Its rules say how this machine is built; they specify no product, and a product on another system would not install them. Architecture's HOW cites them and restates none (`specs.md` §3). |
| F4 | **Fifteen cells force empty ones.** | **Survives, scoped** | Every question applies in every perspective, the three being views of one product (the operator's amend, #723 plan revision 3); a subject that truly lacks a cell says so with `n/a: <reason>`, which the guard requires to carry its reason (`specs.md` §4). |
| F5 | **"statement" clashes with a core term.** | **Refuted** | The Agent vocabulary defines `claim` (Rule-9), not `statement`; `check_craft` refuses any craft term equal to an Agent term and runs on this craft. |
| F6 | **`status: accepted` is a ratification event a Tended Rule cannot declare.** | **Refuted** | It records a done-`Y` already given, read from the row its `row:` line names, and asks nothing (`specs.md` §5); the guard fails an `accepted` whose row holds no `Y done`. |
| F7 | **WHEN with no date cannot say "next week".** | **Survives, scoped** | The operator may give a date, written as a statement cited to the operator's own words; the agent never writes a date of its own (`statements.md` §3). |
| F8 | **A UX/UI WHEN is a moment in the journey, not a row.** | **Survives, scoped** | The moment is the statement's text; the row or release that builds the step is named beside it, so the guard's one WHEN check holds in all three perspectives (#723 plan revision 3). |
| F9 | **The template cannot pass the guard in a system whose ledger lacks its rows.** | **Survives, scoped** | The template cites only the craft's own paths and writes rows as the placeholder `#<id>`, which the guard reads as the form and does not resolve (`../../templates/README.md`). |
| F10 | **An `ext:` citation passes unchecked.** | **Confirmed, stated** | The guard cannot resolve a reference into the world; it warns once per citation, and the reading is inference, as Rule-20 states of every `world` kind. |

**Disposition:** by the done-`Y` of d-work #723 (see ledger #723).
